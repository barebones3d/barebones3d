The Barebones Engineering Standard

Most replacement hardware suffers from massive retail markups, opaque fitment data, and poor metallurgical durability. We engineer around 3 strict principles:

01 / DIAGNOSTIC PRECISION

Calculators Over Marketing

We don't publish generic affiliate reviews. We build interactive tools that compute exact nominal electrical bounds (R = V² / P, capacitance variance, voltage tolerances) so technicians verify before buying.

02 / COMMERCIAL METALLURGY

Industrial Grade Alloys

We advocate for Grade 2 Titanium and Incoloy 800 over cheap copper, and Silicon Nitride over brittle Silicon Carbide—eliminating premature thermal fatigue and scale degradation.

03 / RAPID PROTOTYPING

3D CAD & Obsolete Adapters

Our roots in additive manufacturing allow us to model and specify exact bracket adapters, terminal boots, and custom mounting collars when OEMs discontinue legacy parts.
