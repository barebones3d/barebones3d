## Step-by-Step Installation Procedure
### Step 1: Battery and Bin Release

Press the red lever to release and slide off the clear dust bin. Unscrew the three Torx/Phillips screws securing the battery pack (one on the rear handle spine, two on the base) and drop the battery out.

![Original broken plastic Dyson V10 / V11 Cordless Vacuum Reinforced Trigger Switch Assembly (OEM #970050-01) shown side-by-side with reinforced 3D printed PETG Heavy Duty reproduction.](https://barebones3d.com/assets/parts/970050-01-dyson-v10-v11-cordless-vacuum-reinforced-trigger-switch-970050-01-step-1.webp)

### Step 2: Motor Body Clamshell Separation

Using a long-shank T8 Torx driver, extract the five perimeter screws securing the main handle halves. Disconnect the electrical wire harness leading to the LCD/trigger microswitch board and separate the housing.

![Original broken plastic Dyson V10 / V11 Cordless Vacuum Reinforced Trigger Switch Assembly (OEM #970050-01) shown side-by-side with reinforced 3D printed PETG Heavy Duty reproduction.](https://barebones3d.com/assets/parts/970050-01-dyson-v10-v11-cordless-vacuum-reinforced-trigger-switch-970050-01-step-2.webp)

### Step 3: Broken Trigger Lever Extraction

Locate the red trigger mechanism. The common failure point is the microscopic internal plastic structural rib that contacts the electronic microswitch. Slide the broken trigger out of its retention track along with the small coil spring.

![Original broken plastic Dyson V10 / V11 Cordless Vacuum Reinforced Trigger Switch Assembly (OEM #970050-01) shown side-by-side with reinforced 3D printed PETG Heavy Duty reproduction.](https://barebones3d.com/assets/parts/970050-01-dyson-v10-v11-cordless-vacuum-reinforced-trigger-switch-970050-01-step-3.webp)

### Step 4: Reinforced Trigger Installation and Clamshell Assembly

Position the heavy-duty reinforced trigger into the guide slot, making sure the return coil spring is seated over the guide pin. Re-align the handle clamshell, route the wiring away from pinch points, and re-torque the five T8 screws. Slide the battery pack into place and verify immediate motor response.
