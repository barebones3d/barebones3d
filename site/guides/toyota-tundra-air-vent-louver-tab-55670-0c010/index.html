Step-by-Step Installation Tutorial

3 Steps
STEP 01

Protect Dash and Pry Vent Bezel

Apply painter tape along the dashboard perimeter to protect the soft vinyl. Insert a nylon pry tool at the 3 o clock position and pop the retaining spring tabs outward.

Extract Broken Linkage Remnants

Fish out the broken OEM plastic fingers from inside the horizontal vent slat. Use needle-nose pliers to make sure the internal vertical sync-bar is unblocked.

Slide New ASA Tab Over Center Slat

Position the front grip tab over the central horizontal vane, then press the rear fork into the vertical sync rod until it snaps firmly into the retention notch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Toyota sell this tab separately?
No. Toyota dealerships only sell the entire dashboard register assembly (OEM #55670-0C010) which retails for upwards of $260.00.
Will this melt in the hot summer sun?
No. It is printed in ASA (Acrylonitrile Styrene Acrylate), which withstands temperatures up to 100°C without sagging and contains built-in UV stabilizers.
