## Step-by-Step Installation Procedure
### Step 1: Dishtray Extraction and Wheel Inspection

Pull the lower dishrack completely out of the wash tub and place it inverted on a clean counter. Inspect the eight roller positions to locate melted, flat-spotted, or missing wheels.

### Step 2: Damaged Wheel Clip Separation

Insert the flathead screwdriver blade beneath the central locking tang of the plastic wheel axle bracket. Pry slightly outward while pushing the wheel assembly off the steel wire frame.

![Original broken plastic Whirlpool Dishwasher Lower Dishrack Roller Wheel & Axle Clip (OEM #WPW10195417) shown side-by-side with reinforced 3D printed PETG Heavy Duty reproduction.](https://barebones3d.com/assets/parts/wpw10195417-whirlpool-dishwasher-lower-dishrack-roller-wheel-axle-clip-wpw10195417-step-2.webp)

### Step 3: New Roller Alignment over Wire Frame

Align the replacement wheel assembly with the dual wire rungs. The axle notch must straddle the lower wire while the upper locking clip hooks over the top stabilizing rail.

![Original broken plastic Whirlpool Dishwasher Lower Dishrack Roller Wheel & Axle Clip (OEM #WPW10195417) shown side-by-side with reinforced 3D printed PETG Heavy Duty reproduction.](https://barebones3d.com/assets/parts/wpw10195417-whirlpool-dishwasher-lower-dishrack-roller-wheel-axle-clip-wpw10195417-step-3.webp)

### Step 4: Snap-Lock Engagement and Tub Glide Test

Press inward firmly with your thumb on the wheel axle until the retention barb clicks over the wire. Return the rack to the dishwasher tub and roll it in and out to ensure smooth, unhindered tracking over the tub door bridge.

## Frequently Asked Questions
#### Q: Why is this part listed as affiliate-only instead of print-on-demand?
**A:** OEM appliance counters charge $35 per single roller wheel. However, high-grade injection-molded 8-packs are widely available on Amazon for under $12. Print-on-demand unit economics cannot compete with $1.50/wheel pricing, so we direct owners to mass-market multipacks via affiliate routing while providing free STL files for DIY makers.

#### Q: Can I print this part on my home printer?
**A:** Yes, but you must use PETG, ABS, or Nylon. PLA will soften, warp, and release micro-plastics during the dishwasher's 65°C (150°F) heated wash and sanitize dry cycles.
