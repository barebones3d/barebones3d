Step-by-Step Installation Tutorial

4 Steps
STEP 01

Pry Passenger Dash Trim Strip

Gently unclip the horizontal passenger dashboard trim bezel to expose the two cupholder faceplate screws.

STEP 02

Extract Cupholder Cartridge

Remove the two PH1 screws and slide the entire rectangular cassette forward out of the dashboard housing.

STEP 03

Replace Sheared Heart-Cam Pawl

Pop open the cassette side plate and replace the broken spring-loaded heart-cam latch and pinion.

STEP 04

Reassemble & Test Push-Push Latch

Snap side plate shut, slide cassette back into dashboard cavity, tighten screws, and reinstall dash trim.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did the cup holder fail to pop out?
The factory molded heart-cam follower tooth wears down or shears off, failing to catch the retraction detent.
Is driver side and passenger side identical?
The internal latch pawl is universally mirrored, fitting both left and right dashboard cassettes.
