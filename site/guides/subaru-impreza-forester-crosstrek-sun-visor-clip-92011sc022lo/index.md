## Frequently Asked Questions
#### Q: Why do Subaru visor clips snap constantly?
**A:** Subaru's OEM plastic contains zero UV stabilizers. Sitting directly behind the windshield greenhouse generates interior cabin soak temperatures above 65°C, causing the plastic jaw tabs to snap on simple visor pulls.

#### Q: Can this be printed in PLA?
**A:** Never use PLA inside an automobile cabin. PLA softens at 55°C (131°F) and will deform within 48 hours of summer parking. Automotive ASA maintains dimensional stability past 95°C.
