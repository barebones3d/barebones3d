Step-by-Step Installation Tutorial

4 Steps
STEP 01

Cosmetic Hinge Cap Pry

Insert a thin flathead screwdriver into the small notch on the underside of the visor pivot base. Pop open the plastic hinged cover to expose the mounting screw.

STEP 02

T20 Fastener Extraction and Clip Drop

Back out the single T20 Torx screw securing the bracket to the roof crossmember. Rotate the broken visor clip 90 degrees counter-clockwise to release the internal retention ears from the sheet metal slot.

STEP 03

ASA High-Temp Replacement Block Insertion

Slide the Barebones 3D ASA visor anchor block into the keyed roof opening. Rotate 90 degrees clockwise until the retention shoulders lock behind the sheet metal backing.

STEP 04

Torque Fastener and Snap Engagement Test

Thread the factory T20 screw through the counterbore hole and tighten securely. Fold the visor stalk in and out of the clip jaw to verify positive mechanical retention without binding.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do Subaru visor clips snap constantly?
Subaru's OEM plastic contains zero UV stabilizers. Sitting directly behind the windshield greenhouse generates interior cabin soak temperatures above 65°C, causing the plastic jaw tabs to snap on simple visor pulls.
Can this be printed in PLA?
Never use PLA inside an automobile cabin. PLA softens at 55°C (131°F) and will deform within 48 hours of summer parking. Automotive ASA maintains dimensional stability past 95°C.
