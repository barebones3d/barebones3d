## Step-by-Step Installation Procedure
### Step 1: Unclip Interior Rubber Accordion Bellows

Lower the sunshade fully and place the sunroof into partial vent position if operational. Working from the vehicle interior, wedge a non-marring nylon pry tool beneath the top lip of the rubber accordion boot on each side of the moving glass panel. Carefully peel down the three retaining push-tabs to expose the internal metal glass cradle and mounting hardware.

### Step 2: Remove Torx T25 Fasteners and Lift Glass Panel

Using a magnetic Torx T25 driver, remove the two forward and two rear shoulder screws securing the glass bracket to the mechanical slider arms. Support the glass center with one hand while backing out the final fastener. Lift the glass panel straight up and out through the roof aperture and place it onto a clean, padded surface.

### Step 3: Clear Shattered OEM Plastic Debris from Track Channels

Inspect the extruded aluminum guide rails for broken fragments of the brittle OEM lift arms. Using long reach needle-nose pliers, extract all sheared pivot ears, guide teeth, and retaining clips from the cable carrier channel. Thoroughly vacuum both track channels to ensure no residual plastic grit remains to bind the motor drive cables.

### Step 4: Install Replacement Nylon PA12 Lift Arm Assembly

Apply a liberal coating of synthetic fluoropolymer grease into the guide grooves and pivot pins of the replacement Nylon PA12 arm. Angle the forward guide block into the upper aluminum rail slot, then swing the rear pivot downward to interface with the cable drive carriage. Press downward firmly until the retention snap-tab engages the lower carrier with an audible click.

![Original broken plastic Ford F-150 / Super Duty Sunroof Track Slider Lift Arm Repair Kit (OEM #FL3Z-1651071-A) shown side-by-side with reinforced 3D printed Nylon PA12 reproduction.](https://barebones3d.com/assets/parts/fl3z-1651071-a-ford-f-150-super-duty-sunroof-track-slider-lift-arm-repair-kit-fl3z-1651071-a-step-4.webp)

### Step 5: Remount Glass Panel, Align Seams & Calibrate Motor

Lower the glass panel back onto the lift arms and thread the four T25 Torx screws in finger-tight. Align the front glass edge flush to 1mm below the front roofline, and the rear edge flush to 1mm above the sheet metal to eliminate wind noise, then torque screws to 4.5 Nm. Snap the accordion boots back on. Turn ignition ON, hold the sunroof TILT switch for 10 seconds until the glass cycles down and up to calibrate motor anti-pinch limits.

## Frequently Asked Questions
#### Q: Why did the original Ford factory lift arms crack?
**A:** The factory OEM arms were injection-molded from unreinforced POM/PBT that degrades from heat cycling and UV exposure through the glass. Once the factory track grease collects dust and thickens, the motor drive cable exerts excessive shear force on the brittle lift cam, snapping the guide pin collar.

#### Q: Why is Nylon PA12 required instead of standard PLA or PETG?
**A:** Vehicle cabins parked in direct sunlight reach interior ceiling temperatures between 65°C and 80°C. Standard PLA softens at 55°C, while PETG creeps under spring tension. Industrial Nylon PA12 maintains structural integrity past 140°C and provides natural self-lubricating surface lubricity.

#### Q: Do I need to drop the headliner to perform this repair?
**A:** No. Dealerships charge $1,800 to $2,500 because they drop the headliner to replace the entire 60001 track assembly. Our drop-in replacement arms install from the roof opening once the four T25 glass shoulder bolts and accordion bellows are removed.
