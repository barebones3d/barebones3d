Step-by-Step Installation Tutorial

5 Steps
STEP 01

Release Top Panel Latches

Insert putty knife between front panel and top lid to release the dual internal spring retention latches.

STEP 02

Disconnect Door Switch & Bulkhead

Unplug door safety interlock wiring harness and remove 1/4 inch screws securing front bearing bulkhead.

STEP 03

Pry Out Worn Slide Shoes

Pop out worn Teflon/nylon slide glides from their retention slots in the upper half-circle bearing bracket.

STEP 04

Snap In New Glides & Felt Seal

Snap new white/green slides into place and align the felt seal cushion along the outer rim.

STEP 05

Reassemble Bulkhead & Spin Test

Remount front panel, reconnect door sensor, lower top lid, and spin drum by hand to verify silent rotation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What causes the loud screeching during dryer cycles?
When the plastic glides wear through, the metal drum lip grinds directly on the stationary bulkhead.
