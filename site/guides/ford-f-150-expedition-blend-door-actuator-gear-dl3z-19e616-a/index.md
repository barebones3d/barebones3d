### Step 1: Center Stack Side Trim Release

Insert a non-marring pry bar into the lower seam of the center console trim panel on the passenger side. Pop the metal spring clips outward to reveal the lower HVAC plenum casing. Disconnect the auxiliary 12V socket harness.

## Frequently Asked Questions
#### Q: What causes the rapid clicking sound behind my F-150 radio?
**A:** When the HVAC door reaches physical travel limits, the motor continues to supply torque. The weak factory POM gear teeth shear off, causing the worm gear to skip rapidly across the stripped void.

#### Q: Do I have to remove the entire dash to fix this?
**A:** No. Dealership flat rate pays for complete dashboard drop, but with a 5.5mm flex-head ratchet, this sub-gear replacement can be completed through the passenger glovebox cavity in under an hour.
