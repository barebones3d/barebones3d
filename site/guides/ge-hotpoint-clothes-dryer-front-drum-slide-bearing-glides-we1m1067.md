## Frequently Asked Questions
#### Q: What causes the loud screeching during dryer cycles?
**A:** When the plastic glides wear through, the metal drum lip grinds directly on the stationary bulkhead.

#### Q: Should I print these or buy the Amazon multipack?
**A:** Amazon sells 4-packs with replacement felt and adhesive for under $8, making commercial POD uneconomical.
