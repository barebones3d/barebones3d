## Frequently Asked Questions
#### Q: Why is a terminal protector essential for Makita 18V batteries?
**A:** Makita 18V LXT battery terminal blades are exposed during transport in toolboxes and trucks. Accidental shorting against loose fasteners or keys can damage the internal protection PCB or cause a fire.
