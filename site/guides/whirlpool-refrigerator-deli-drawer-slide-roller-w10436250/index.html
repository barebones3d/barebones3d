Step-by-Step Installation Tutorial

3 Steps
STEP 01

Remove Deli Drawer Pan

Slide the deli meat pan all the way out and lift upward at a 30-degree angle to clear the front stop catches.

STEP 02

Extract Sheared Roller Wheel

Pry the plastic axle pin with a small flathead tool and pop the broken roller wheel out of the drawer guide bracket.

STEP 03

Install New Roller and Axle

Press the replacement roller wheel over the axle pin and push into the mounting hole until the clip snaps flush.
