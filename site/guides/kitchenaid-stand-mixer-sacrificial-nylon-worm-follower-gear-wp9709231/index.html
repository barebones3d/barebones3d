Step-by-Step Installation Tutorial

5 Steps
STEP 01

Remove Trim Band and Planetary Pin

Unscrew decorative chrome belly band and use 1/8 inch punch to drive out planetary lock pin.

STEP 02

Open Upper Gear Housing

Remove four housing screws and separate top motor housing to expose the transmission gear box.

STEP 03

Extract Stripped Nylon Gear

Slide off the bronze washer and lift out the stripped sacrificial nylon follower gear.

STEP 04

Repack Grease & Install New Gear

Pack gear teeth with NSF food-grade grease and seat replacement worm gear over drive tower.

STEP 05

Realign Planetary & Fasten Casing

Rejoin housing, reinsert and stake roll pin, and run mixer on speed 1 for 2 minutes to distribute grease.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is this gear intentionally made of nylon?
It acts as a mechanical fuse. If dough hooks jam, the gear strips to protect the costly electric drive motor.
Why choose the Amazon kit over custom 3D printing?
Injection-molded replacement gears packaged with NSF food-grade grease are available on Amazon for under $10.
