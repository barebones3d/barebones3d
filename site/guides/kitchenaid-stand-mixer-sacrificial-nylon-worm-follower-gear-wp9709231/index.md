## Frequently Asked Questions
#### Q: Why is this gear intentionally made of nylon?
**A:** It acts as a mechanical fuse. If dough hooks jam, the gear strips to protect the costly electric drive motor.

#### Q: Why choose the Amazon kit over custom 3D printing?
**A:** Injection-molded replacement gears packaged with NSF food-grade grease are available on Amazon for under $10.
