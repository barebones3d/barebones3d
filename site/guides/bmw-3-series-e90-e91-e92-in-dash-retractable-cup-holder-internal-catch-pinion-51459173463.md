## Frequently Asked Questions
#### Q: Why did the cup holder fail to pop out?
**A:** The factory molded heart-cam follower tooth wears down or shears off, failing to catch the retraction detent.

#### Q: Is driver side and passenger side identical?
**A:** The internal latch pawl is universally mirrored, fitting both left and right dashboard cassettes.
