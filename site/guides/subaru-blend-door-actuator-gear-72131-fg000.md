## Step-by-Step Installation Procedure
### Step 1: Lower Dash & Glovebox Removal

Depress the side damper clips inside the passenger glovebox. Unscrew the two lower pivot screws using a #2 Phillips screwdriver. Gently pull downward to release the assembly without stressing the nylon hinges.

> **Pro Tip:** Place screws into an organizer tray to avoid mixing up trim screws with chassis ground screws.

### Step 2: Locate HVAC Blend Door Actuator

Look to the left of the cabin air filter cavity on the HVAC climate box. You will see a small black plastic servo motor box secured by three 7mm hex bolts.

> **Caution:** Do not force the blend door linkage arm by hand while the motor is plugged in to avoid stripping the interior potentiometer.

![Original broken plastic Subaru HVAC Blend Door Actuator Split Gear (OEM #72131-FG000) shown side-by-side with reinforced 3D printed Nylon PA12 reproduction.](https://barebones3d.com/assets/parts/72131-fg000-subaru-blend-door-actuator-gear-72131-fg000-step-2.webp)

### Step 3: Unclip Wire Harness & Extract Actuator

Depress the locking tab on the 5-pin wiring connector and extract it. Remove the three 7mm bolts and lift the actuator motor straight outward from the HVAC casing.

![Original broken plastic Subaru HVAC Blend Door Actuator Split Gear (OEM #72131-FG000) shown side-by-side with reinforced 3D printed Nylon PA12 reproduction.](https://barebones3d.com/assets/parts/72131-fg000-subaru-blend-door-actuator-gear-72131-fg000-step-3.webp)

### Step 4: Open Actuator Case & Swap Split Gear

Pry the four perimeter casing clips with a flat tool. The OEM white nylon spur gear will likely show a hairline fracture along the D-bore shaft. Clean out old dried grease, apply fresh white lithium grease, and press the new Barebones 3D PA12 Nylon gear onto the metal drive shaft.

> **Pro Tip:** Nylon PA12 provides self-lubricating qualities and higher shear strength than standard injection-molded POM.

![Original broken plastic Subaru HVAC Blend Door Actuator Split Gear (OEM #72131-FG000) shown side-by-side with reinforced 3D printed Nylon PA12 reproduction.](https://barebones3d.com/assets/parts/72131-fg000-subaru-blend-door-actuator-gear-72131-fg000-step-4.webp)

### Step 5: Re-indexing & Functional Climate Test

Snap the casing together, re-seat onto the HVAC box spline, reconnect the 5-pin plug, and turn vehicle ignition to ON. Cycle cabin temperature from Cold (60°F) to Hot (85°F) to verify smooth, quiet flap travel before reinstalling the glovebox.

## Frequently Asked Questions
#### Q: Why does the OEM Subaru gear fail?
**A:** The OEM gear was molded from brittle unreinforced POM with an undersized wall thickness around the drive D-flat. Cyclic thermal expansion between winter heating and summer A/C causes radial hoop stress cracking.

#### Q: Why is Nylon PA12 specified instead of PLA or PETG?
**A:** Automotive under-dash temperatures can exceed 70°C in direct summer sunlight. PLA deforms at 55°C. PA12 Nylon has a heat deflection temperature of over 110°C, high impact resistance, and natural lubricity.

#### Q: Does this part require special slicer tolerances?
**A:** The free STL is engineered with a +0.15mm printer tolerance compensation for FDM. If printing on a Bambu or Prusa, slice at 0.12mm layer height and 100% infill.
