## Frequently Asked Questions
#### Q: Do I need to desolder any audio wires?
**A:** No. The hinge is engineered with a slotted wire passthrough channel that eliminates desoldering completely.

#### Q: Why is SLS Nylon PA12 required instead of resin?
**A:** Resin becomes brittle and snaps under the torsional flexing of the headband. Nylon PA12 provides flex fatigue resistance.
