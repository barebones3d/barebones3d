Step-by-Step Installation Tutorial

5 Steps
STEP 01

Remove Inner Hinge Cover Screws

Unscrew the two Torx T6 micro screws on the inner headband hinge clamp to expose audio wiring.

STEP 02

Unhook Swivel Ear-Cup Arm

Carefully slide the cracked outer swivel knuckle off the steel headband slider arm without pinching the wire.

STEP 03

Thread Audio Wire Through New Hinge

Using tweezers, guide the audio wire harness safely through the internal slot of the Nylon PA12 hinge.

STEP 04

Snap Hinge Knuckle & Tighten Screws

Seat the hinge knuckle into the slider track and torque the two T6 screws snugly until flush.

STEP 05

Verify Fold & Audio Continuity

Fold earcup 90 degrees flat into storage orientation and power on headset to verify stereo audio channels.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to desolder any audio wires?
No. The hinge is engineered with a slotted wire passthrough channel that eliminates desoldering completely.
Why is SLS Nylon PA12 required instead of resin?
Resin becomes brittle and snaps under the torsional flexing of the headband. Nylon PA12 provides flex fatigue resistance.
