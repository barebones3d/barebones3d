## Frequently Asked Questions
#### Q: Why do washing machine filter handles snap off?
**A:** Lint, hair, and hard water scale bind the rubber seal against the plastic pump housing. When users attempt to force it open with pliers, the thin center web shears off.

#### Q: Does this replace the whole filter or just the handle?
**A:** This is a slip-on mechanical sleeve that interlocks with the surviving filter core, saving you from purchasing an expensive replacement pump assembly.
