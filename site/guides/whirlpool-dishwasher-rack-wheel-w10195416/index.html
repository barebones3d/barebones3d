Step-by-Step Installation Tutorial

4 Steps
STEP 01

Extract Lower Dishwasher Rack

Open the dishwasher door fully, roll the lower rack out, and lift it clear of the tub rails. Place it inverted on a clean kitchen towel.

STEP 02

Inspect and Remove Broken Snap Retainer

Examine the wheel bracket. The OEM retention tab becomes chalky and brittle from caustic detergent. Gently slide a flat screwdriver behind the tab and push toward the wire frame to unclip.

STEP 03

Clean Wire Frame Mounting Node

Wipe any calcium or mineral scale off the stainless steel wire rack node with white vinegar or a scouring pad to ensure smooth wheel rotation.

STEP 04

Snap-fit Heavy-Duty Replacement Assembly

Align the inner axle channel with the rack wire, press firmly until an audible click confirms engagement, then slide the wheel onto the hub. Verify free spinning without wobble.
