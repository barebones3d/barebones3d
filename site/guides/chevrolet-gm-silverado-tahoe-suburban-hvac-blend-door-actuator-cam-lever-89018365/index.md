## Frequently Asked Questions
#### Q: Why does the blend door continuously click?
**A:** The split D-bore slips on the fluted metal shaft; the motor senses no resistance and keeps rotating indefinitely.

#### Q: Does this require pulling the entire dashboard?
**A:** No. Dealerships book 6+ hours dashboard removal, but this lever presses on from the footwell.
