Step-by-Step Installation Tutorial

5 Steps
STEP 01

Drop Lower Passenger Footwell Cover

Remove the three plastic trim retainers and swing the lower insulator panel down to expose the HVAC airbox.

STEP 02

Unbolt Blend Door Actuator

Disconnect electrical connector and remove the two 5.5mm screws securing the blend door actuator.

STEP 03

Inspect & Extract Split D-Bore Cam

Pull the cracked factory cam lever off the fluted blend door shaft and inspect splines for debris.

STEP 04

Press Fit Reinforced Nylon PA12 Cam

Align keyway and press the reinforced Nylon PA12 cam firmly over the fluted blend door axle.

STEP 05

Reinstall Actuator & Run HVAC Re-learn

Torque screws, plug harness, pull HVAC fuse for 60s, then start engine and allow auto-calibration.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the blend door continuously click?
The split D-bore slips on the fluted metal shaft; the motor senses no resistance and keeps rotating indefinitely.
Does this require pulling the entire dashboard?
No. Dealerships book 6+ hours dashboard removal, but this lever presses on from the footwell.
