## Step-by-Step Installation Procedure
### Step 1: Knee Bolster Drop and Duct Clearance

Remove the four 7mm hex screws securing the driver-side lower dash knee bolster trim panel. Pop the metal spring clips free and set the bolster aside. Unhook the lower footwell floor air discharge duct to reveal the HVAC case side.

### Step 2: Actuator Extraction and Blind Fastener Access

Unplug the electrical connector from the defrost/mode actuator. Using a 5.5mm flex micro-ratchet, remove the two mounting screws. Pull the actuator straight off the airbox flapper shaft.

### Step 3: Sheared Cam Lever Extraction

Inspect the output drive. The original white POM cam lever cracks across the splined hub keyway, spinning freely on the shaft. Pull the broken cam off the airbox flapper door shaft with pliers and clean the splined pin.

![Original broken plastic GM GMT900 HVAC Recirculation & Defrost Blend Door Actuator Cam Lever (OEM #20826188) shown side-by-side with reinforced 3D printed Nylon PA12 reproduction.](https://barebones3d.com/assets/parts/20826188-gm-gmt900-hvac-recirculation-defrost-blend-door-actuator-cam-lever-20826188-step-3.webp)

### Step 4: PA12 Cam Installation and GM Auto-Calibration

Press the Barebones 3D PA12 splined cam lever onto the flapper shaft until seated against the shoulder stop. Align the cam track pin with the actuator guide slot. Bolt the actuator down, reconnect the harness, pull the HVAC/ECU B fuse for 1 minute, and turn the key ON for a 90-second automated recalibration sweep.

## Frequently Asked Questions
#### Q: Why does air stay stuck blowing only out of the dashboard defrost vents?
**A:** GM's default safety fail-position for the GMT900 HVAC distribution case is full windshield defrost. When the plastic cam lever snaps, spring tension defaults the air door to the windshield.

#### Q: What is the dealer quote for this repair?
**A:** GM dealer technicians frequently claim the entire air distribution case must be replaced when the internal cam shears, quoting $1,200 to $1,600 involving complete instrument panel removal.
