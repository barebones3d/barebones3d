## Step-by-Step Installation Procedure
### Step 1: Inspect Tonearm Pivot Housing

Locate the small horizontal hole at the rear right of the tonearm gimbal bearing. Inspect for remnants of the snapped plastic guide arm.

![Original broken plastic Vintage Pioneer PL-12D Tonearm Anti-Skate Weight Guide Arm (OEM #WA-104) shown side-by-side with reinforced 3D printed PETG reproduction.](https://barebones3d.com/assets/parts/wa-104-pioneer-turntable-anti-skate-arm-wa-104-step-1.webp)

### Step 2: Install Replacement Guide Wire Arm

Insert the printed guide wire stem into the gimbal receiver. Tighten the side grub screw lightly with an Allen key until snug.

![Original broken plastic Vintage Pioneer PL-12D Tonearm Anti-Skate Weight Guide Arm (OEM #WA-104) shown side-by-side with reinforced 3D printed PETG reproduction.](https://barebones3d.com/assets/parts/wa-104-pioneer-turntable-anti-skate-arm-wa-104-step-2.webp)

### Step 3: Re-hang Weight and Calibrate Skating Force

Thread monofilament line through the notched guide wire and suspend the brass anti-skate weight according to your cartridge tracking force (typically notch 2 for 1.5g tracking force).
