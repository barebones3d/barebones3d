Step-by-Step Installation Tutorial

3 Steps
STEP 01

Inspect Tonearm Pivot Housing

Locate the small horizontal hole at the rear right of the tonearm gimbal bearing. Inspect for remnants of the snapped plastic guide arm.

STEP 02

Install Replacement Guide Wire Arm

Insert the printed guide wire stem into the gimbal receiver. Tighten the side grub screw lightly with an Allen key until snug.

STEP 03

Re-hang Weight and Calibrate Skating Force

Thread monofilament line through the notched guide wire and suspend the brass anti-skate weight according to your cartridge tracking force (typically notch 2 for 1.5g tracking force).
