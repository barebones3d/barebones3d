Step-by-Step Installation Tutorial

4 Steps
STEP 01

Ear Pad and Inner Dust Scrim Removal

Grip the edge of the leatherette ear cushion and pull firmly inward to disengage the eight perimeter plastic locking tabs. Peel back the foam inner scrim to inspect the swivel joint wiring channel.

STEP 02

Headband Swivel Bracket Screws Extraction

Locate the two PH00 screws securing the plastic interior hinge cover plate. Back them out with your precision driver and set them on a magnetic mat. Pry the cosmetic outer shell free with a spudger.

STEP 03

Broken Swivel Arm Demounting and Wiring Clearance

Carefully tilt the cracked hinge collar off the steel headband slider pin. Slide the delicate audio cable through the hinge slot with tweezers. Take care not to pinch the braided shield or pull solder joints loose from the ear-cup driver board.

STEP 04

PA12 Replacement Hinge Seat and Mechanical Closure

Thread the audio harness through the open slot of the Barebones 3D PA12 replacement swivel arm. Snap the arm over the metal pivot pin, align the cosmetic backplate, and torque the two PH00 screws until snug. Re-clip the ear cushions until you hear the eight distinct clicks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will Bose repair just the plastic hinge collar?
No. Bose considers the hinge non-serviceable and will only offer an out-of-warranty replacement unit for $150 to $200+, rendering this $19 repair an 88% cost savings.
Why is Nylon PA12 necessary instead of PLA or resin?
The swivel hinge undergoes continuous torsional twisting and tension every time the headphones are flexed onto the listener's head. PLA creeps and fractures along layer lines, while SLS Nylon PA12 provides isotropic mechanical strength.
