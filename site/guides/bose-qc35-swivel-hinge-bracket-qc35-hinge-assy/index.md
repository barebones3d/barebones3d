## Frequently Asked Questions
#### Q: Will Bose repair just the plastic hinge collar?
**A:** No. Bose considers the hinge non-serviceable and will only offer an out-of-warranty replacement unit for $150 to $200+, rendering this $19 repair an 88% cost savings.

#### Q: Why is Nylon PA12 necessary instead of PLA or resin?
**A:** The swivel hinge undergoes continuous torsional twisting and tension every time the headphones are flexed onto the listener's head. PLA creeps and fractures along layer lines, while SLS Nylon PA12 provides isotropic mechanical strength.
