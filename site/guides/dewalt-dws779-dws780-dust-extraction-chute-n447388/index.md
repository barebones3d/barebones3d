## Frequently Asked Questions
#### Q: Why does the factory DeWalt dust chute perform so poorly?
**A:** The stock rubber chute sits over 3 inches back from the blade kerf and flexes away under suction, catching less than 40% of waste stream particles. This rigid, contoured scoop captures upward of 90%.

#### Q: Will this part melt from blade friction or hot carbide teeth?
**A:** No. The chute is printed in industrial PETG with a 75°C+ heat deflection temperature and is engineered with clearance to maintain an intentional 8mm air gap away from high-speed carbide tooth orbit.
