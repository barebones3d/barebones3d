## Frequently Asked Questions
#### Q: Can I replace latches without touching the internal lithium cells?
**A:** Yes. The latches reside in an isolated side perimeter channel; internal cell connections remain untouched.

#### Q: Why is Nylon PA12 necessary for power tool latches?
**A:** Power tools endure repeated high-vibration shocks and job site drops that will instantly fracture standard PLA or resin.
