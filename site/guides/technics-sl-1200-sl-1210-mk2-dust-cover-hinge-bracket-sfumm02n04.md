## Frequently Asked Questions
#### Q: Why did Panasonic discontinue this part?
**A:** Panasonic phased out legacy SL-1200MK2 component spares in the early 2010s. OEM new-old-stock pairs now trade on vintage Hi-Fi marketplaces for over $100.

#### Q: Why is ASA used instead of standard black ABS?
**A:** Automotive ASA delivers the high impact strength of ABS but contains an acrylate elastomer that provides total immunity to UV yellowing and solar degradation from room sunlight.
