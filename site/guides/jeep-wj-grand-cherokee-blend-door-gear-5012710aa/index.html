Step-by-Step Installation Tutorial

4 Steps
STEP 01

Glove Box Drop and Airbox Visual Access

Open the glove compartment door, press inward on the plastic stops on both left and right sides to disengage the travel limiters, and rotate the door completely downward. Locate the passenger-side blend door actuator mounted directly to the HVAC housing sub-frame.

STEP 02

Actuator Fastener Removal and Harness Disconnect

Disconnect the 2-pin electrical connector by depressing the central tab. Use an 8mm low-profile ratchet wrench to back out the two retaining hex head bolts securing the actuator bracket to the airbox. Carefully lower the assembly straight down to clear the D-shaped door drive shaft.

STEP 03

Clamshell Separation and Cracked POM Gear Extraction

Insert a precision flat blade into the four perimeter plastic locking clips along the seam of the actuator housing. Separate the top case to expose the reduction gear train. Extract the large split drive gear from the metal motor worm gear spindle, noting the internal shaft key orientation.

STEP 04

PA12 Replacement Gear Press-Fit and Re-indexing

Apply synthetic gear grease to the gear teeth. Align the Barebones 3D PA12 replacement gear bore onto the center metal drive post. Press the gear firmly into mesh with the intermediate drive pinions. Snap the clamshell cover shut until all tabs click, reinstall the actuator with the 8mm bolts, and perform the WJ dual-zone climate calibration sequence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did the original gear split in half?
The factory Chrysler gear is molded from unfilled polyoxymethylene (POM). Constant engine bay thermal cycling embrittles the plastic, while motor stall-torque at travel endpoints induces hoop-stress fractures at the hub.
How do I run the HVAC diagnostic calibration on the WJ?
Turn the ignition ON. Set both driver and passenger temp knobs to 75°F. Simultaneously press and hold the 'A/C' and 'Recirc' buttons while turning the left temperature control knob one click clockwise. Release both buttons.
