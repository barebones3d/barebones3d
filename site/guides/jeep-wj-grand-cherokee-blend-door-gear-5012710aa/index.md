## Frequently Asked Questions
#### Q: Why does the OEM Jeep WJ gear crack?
**A:** The factory injection-molded nylon gear uses thin web walls adjacent to the D-flat shaft interface. Motor stall torque at end-stops combined with summer dashboard heat cycles causes catastrophic hoop stress cracking.

#### Q: How does the +0.15mm tolerance compensation help?
**A:** The D-bore is pre-widened by exactly +0.15mm to guarantee a snug hand press-fit over the motor steel shaft without binding.
