Step-by-Step Installation Tutorial

4 Steps
STEP 01

Disconnect Power & Open Service Flap

Unplug washing machine. Place towels beneath the lower right circular service flap and flip it downward.

STEP 02

Drain Water via Small Bleed Hose

Pull out the small emergency rubber drain hose, remove the plug into your shallow tray, and let all stagnant water drain out.

STEP 03

Slide Barebones 3D Heavy-Duty Handle Over Filter Core

Slide the contoured repair cap over the sheared OEM filter stub. The dual internal rib channels lock firmly into the OEM filter ribs, providing 3x the rotational torque.

STEP 04

Unscrew Counter-Clockwise and Clear Debris

Grip the ergonomic textured handle and turn counter-clockwise 90 degrees. Remove coins, lint, or hairpins from the impeller cavity, rinse the filter, and screw back into place.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do washing machine filter handles snap off?
Lint, hair, and hard water scale bind the rubber seal against the plastic pump housing. When users attempt to force it open with pliers, the thin center web shears off.
Does this replace the whole filter or just the handle?
This is a slip-on mechanical sleeve that interlocks with the surviving filter core, saving you from purchasing an expensive replacement pump assembly.
