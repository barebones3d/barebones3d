Frequently Asked Questions

Why do washing machine filter handles snap off?
Lint, hair, and hard water scale bind the rubber seal against the plastic pump housing. When users attempt to force it open with pliers, the thin center web shears off.
Does this replace the whole filter or just the handle?
This is a slip-on mechanical sleeve that interlocks with the surviving filter core, saving you from purchasing an expensive replacement pump assembly.
