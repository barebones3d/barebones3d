## Frequently Asked Questions
#### Q: Why use ASA instead of PLA?
**A:** Headliners reach over 75°C in direct sun. Standard PLA deforms at 55°C, while Automotive ASA resists 100°C.

#### Q: Does this require cutting visor wiring?
**A:** No. The bracket features a precision slip-channel to clear the vanity mirror wiring harness.
