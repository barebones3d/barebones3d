## Step-by-Step Installation Procedure
### Step 1: Bellows Detachment and Moving Glass Removal

Place the sunroof in the vent position. From inside the cabin, gently pull the rubber side accordion bellows inward to release them from the lower track channel. Use a T25 Torx driver to remove the three retaining bolts on each side of the glass panel. Carefully lift the sunroof glass up and out of the vehicle.

### Step 2: Broken Lift Arm Fragment Extraction

Locate the fractured glass lift arm inside the aluminum extrusion rail. Cycle the sunroof motor slightly if necessary to draw the drive cable slider into view. Extract the broken rear cam slider fragments and vacuum the aluminum channel clean of plastic debris.

![Original broken plastic Ford F-150 / Super Duty Vista Sunroof Track Slider Lift Arm (OEM #FL3Z-16502C22-E) shown side-by-side with reinforced 3D printed Nylon PA12 reproduction.](https://barebones3d.com/assets/parts/fl3z-16502c22-e-ford-f-150-super-duty-vista-sunroof-track-slider-lift-arm-fl3z-16502c22-e-step-2.webp)

### Step 3: PA12 Billet Slider Seating and Cable Pin Engagement

Coat the track slides with Krytox grease. Align the Barebones 3D reinforced Nylon PA12 lift arm into the track slot. Guide the drive cable steel drive lug directly into the slider's reinforced pivot pocket until fully engaged.

![Original broken plastic Ford F-150 / Super Duty Vista Sunroof Track Slider Lift Arm (OEM #FL3Z-16502C22-E) shown side-by-side with reinforced 3D printed Nylon PA12 reproduction.](https://barebones3d.com/assets/parts/fl3z-16502c22-e-ford-f-150-super-duty-vista-sunroof-track-slider-lift-arm-fl3z-16502c22-e-step-3.webp)

### Step 4: Glass Re-Installation and Sunroof Module Initialization

Carefully lower the glass back into place and finger-tighten the six T25 Torx screws. Align glass gaps flush with roof sheet metal, then torque to 4.5 Nm. Perform the Ford sunroof initialization: press and hold the TILT button until the sunroof completes a full down-tilt-slide cycle.
