Step-by-Step Installation Tutorial

4 Steps
STEP 01

Dust Cover Separation and Remnant Removal

Lift the acrylic dust cover vertically off the chassis. Back out the two JIS #2 screws securing the broken die-cast/plastic lower hinge receiver bracket from the rear aluminum plinth.

STEP 02

Torsion Spring Pin Transfer

Use needle-nose pliers to extract the internal steel pivot pin and spring from the damaged original housing. Inspect the spring tangs for corrosion or fatigue bending, and wipe clean.

STEP 03

ASA Bracket Base Installation

Position the Barebones 3D UV-resistant ASA replacement hinge bracket into the rear plinth recess. Secure the base using the factory metric M3/M4 JIS screws, making sure the mount is seated squarely against the plinth.

STEP 04

Acrylic Cover Insertion and Hinge Tension Check

Load the steel torsion spring into the ASA bracket socket and insert the hinge slide tab. Slide the acrylic dust cover tongue straight down into the receiver until it seats. Test open/close holding tension at a 45-degree angle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did Panasonic discontinue this part?
Panasonic phased out legacy SL-1200MK2 component spares in the early 2010s. OEM new-old-stock pairs now trade on vintage Hi-Fi marketplaces for over $100.
Why is ASA used instead of standard black ABS?
Automotive ASA delivers the high impact strength of ABS but contains an acrylate elastomer that provides total immunity to UV yellowing and solar degradation from room sunlight.
