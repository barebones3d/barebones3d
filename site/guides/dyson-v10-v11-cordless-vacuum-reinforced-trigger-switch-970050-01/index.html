Step-by-Step Installation Tutorial

4 Steps
STEP 01

Battery and Bin Release

Press the red lever to release and slide off the clear dust bin. Unscrew the three Torx/Phillips screws securing the battery pack (one on the rear handle spine, two on the base) and drop the battery out.

STEP 02

Motor Body Clamshell Separation

Using a long-shank T8 Torx driver, extract the five perimeter screws securing the main handle halves. Disconnect the electrical wire harness leading to the LCD/trigger microswitch board and separate the housing.

STEP 03

Broken Trigger Lever Extraction

Locate the red trigger mechanism. The common failure point is the microscopic internal plastic structural rib that contacts the electronic microswitch. Slide the broken trigger out of its retention track along with the small coil spring.

STEP 04

Reinforced Trigger Installation and Clamshell Assembly

Position the heavy-duty reinforced trigger into the guide slot, making sure the return coil spring is seated over the guide pin. Re-align the handle clamshell, route the wiring away from pinch points, and re-torque the five T8 screws. Slide the battery pack into place and verify immediate motor response.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the OEM hostage gouge on Dyson triggers?
Dyson refuses to sell the 2-gram plastic trigger switch by itself. Dyson service centers require purchasing the entire Main Body & Cyclone motor assembly for $99 to $149.
